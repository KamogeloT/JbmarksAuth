# App Store Description for SDINMOTION

## 📱 App Description (Full Version)

```
SDINMOTION is the official mobile app for JBmarks Local Municipality, empowering residents to quickly and easily report municipal service issues directly from their smartphones.

KEY FEATURES:

📸 Photo Documentation
Take high-quality photos of faults and issues directly from the app to help municipal workers identify and resolve problems faster.

📍 Precise Location Tracking
Automatic GPS location detection ensures your reports are accurately logged, helping municipal teams locate issues quickly.

🏘️ City-Specific Reporting
Select your city (Potchefstroom or Ventersdorp) to ensure your report reaches the correct municipal department.

🔧 Four Service Categories
• Water & Sanitation - Report leaks, blockages, and supply issues
• Electricity - Report outages, faulty streetlights, and electrical hazards
• Roads & Stormwater - Report potholes, damaged signs, and drainage problems
• Waste Management - Report missed collections and illegal dumping

📋 Report History
Track all your submitted reports and their status in one convenient location.

💾 Offline Support
Start a report even without internet connection. Your report will be saved and submitted automatically when you're back online.

⚡ Quick & Easy
Simple, intuitive interface makes reporting municipal issues faster than ever before.

Your reports are automatically routed to the appropriate municipal department for quick resolution, helping improve service delivery in your community.

For support, contact:
Email: support@municipality.gov.za
Phone: +27 18 297 5111
```
