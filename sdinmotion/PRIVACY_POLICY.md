# Privacy Policy for SDINMOTION

**Last Updated:** November 10, 2025

## Introduction

SDINMOTION ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SDINMOTION mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a fault report through the App, we collect:

- **Personal Information:** Your full name, contact number, and email address (optional)
- **Report Details:** Description of the issue, type of fault, and location/address
- **Photos:** Images you choose to attach to your report (optional)

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

- **Location Data:** With your permission, we collect your GPS coordinates to accurately log the location of reported faults
- **Device Information:** Basic device information for app functionality and troubleshooting

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Process and Track Fault Reports:** Submit your reports to the appropriate municipal department
2. **Communicate:** Contact you regarding the status of your reports
3. **Improve Services:** Analyze fault patterns to improve municipal service delivery
4. **Provide Support:** Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

## Permissions Explained

### Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

**Why we need it:** To allow you to take photos of municipal faults directly from the App.

**How we use it:** 
- Photos are captured only when you choose to take a picture
- Photos are attached to your fault report and submitted to the municipality
- We do not access your camera without your explicit action
- Photos are not used for any other purpose

### Location Permission (android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

**Why we need it:** To accurately capture the location of reported faults.

**How we use it:**
- Location is captured only when you choose to use the "Use My Current Location" feature
- GPS coordinates help municipal workers locate and address the reported issue
- You can manually enter an address if you prefer not to share your location
- Location data is not tracked continuously

### Storage/Photo Library Permission (android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

**Why we need it:** To allow you to select existing photos from your device's gallery.

**How we use it:**
- Access is granted only when you choose to upload a photo from your gallery
- We only access the specific photo you select
- We do not access, view, or store any other photos from your device

### Internet Permission (android.permission.INTERNET)

**Why we need it:** To submit your fault reports to the municipal system.

**How we use it:**
- Send your reports to the municipality's task management system (Bitrix24)
- The App requires internet connectivity to function

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Who We Share Your Data With

- **Municipal Authorities:** Your fault reports (including name, contact details, photos, and location) are shared with JBmarks Local Municipality and the relevant municipal departments to address the reported issues.

- **Third-Party Services:** We use Bitrix24 as our task management and reporting system. Your data is transmitted securely to Bitrix24 servers for processing.

### We Do NOT:

- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your photos for marketing purposes
- Track your location when you're not using the App
- Access your camera or photo library without your permission

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage:** Your report history is stored locally on your device for your reference
- **Secure Transmission:** All data transmitted to our servers uses secure HTTPS connections
- **Data Retention:** Reports are retained as long as necessary to address the municipal issue and for record-keeping purposes

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access:** View your report history within the App
- **Opt-Out:** Decline to provide optional information (email, photos)
- **Revoke Permissions:** Change app permissions in your device settings at any time
- **Delete:** Clear your local report history from the App settings

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have rights including:

- Right to correction of inaccurate information
- Right to deletion of information (subject to legal requirements)
- Right to object to processing
