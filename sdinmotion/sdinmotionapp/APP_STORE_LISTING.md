## Full Description
JBMarks Community Reporter is the official fault reporting app for JB Marks Local Municipality (Potchefstroom & Ventersdorp). Report issues directly to the municipality and track their resolution in real-time.

**What you can report:**
🚰 Water & Sanitation — Leaks, burst pipes, no water supply, sewer blockages
⚡ Electricity — Power outages, faulty streetlights, exposed cables
🛣️ Roads & Stormwater — Potholes, damaged signs, flooding, blocked drains
🗑️ Refuse & Waste — Missed collections, illegal dumping, bin damage

**Key Features:**
✅ Quick & easy fault reporting — no account required
📍 GPS location detection for accurate fault pinpointing
📷 Photo capture to show the issue clearly
🔍 Track your report status in real-time
📋 Get a reference number for every report
🏘️ Community feed — see what's being reported in your area
📢 Service announcements — planned maintenance & load shedding
🚨 Emergency contacts — one-tap calling for urgent issues
⭐ Rate the service once your issue is resolved
💾 Offline support — save reports when there's no signal

**How it works:**
1. Select the type of issue
2. Fill in your details and describe the problem
3. Add a photo and GPS location
4. Submit — you'll get a reference number immediately
5. Track the progress until resolution

**Coverage:**
• Potchefstroom
• Ventersdorp
• Surrounding areas within JB Marks Local Municipality
